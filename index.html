Base Camp Incubator

A 3-Phase structured development protocol for high-potential operators. Your roadmap from certification to floor command.

The Architect's Note

"Certification teaches you the movements. The Incubator teaches you how to command the room. We don't throw you to the wolves; we build your armor first. Follow the phases, clear the gates, and earn the floor."

PHASE 01

The Silent Sponge

2-3 Sessions

You do not teach. You observe. Your goal is to map the energetic flow of a live class and study how senior instructors manage chaos.

Directives

  • • Shadow 2 full classes (Lead by Architect or Senior).
  • • Log 3 "Wolf Cues" (Safety) you heard.
  • • Log 3 "Shepherd Cues" (Flow) you heard.

Clearance Gate

Submit your 6 logged cues to the Architect. Successfully identify why the instructor chose to use them in those specific moments.

PHASE 02

The Co-Pilot

2-3 Sessions

You step onto the floor, but the training wheels are on. You will lead a small, controlled segment of a live class under direct supervision.

Directives

  • • Architect leads the first 35 minutes.
  • • You take over and execute a 10-minute pre-approved sequence.
  • • Architect leads the final 5-minute Unwind.

Clearance Gate

Execute the 10-minute block with zero biomechanical safety violations (The Iron Skeleton is maintained).

PHASE 03

The Audited Flight

1 Session

You have the room. You program the class. You execute the flow. The Architect observes from the back.

Directives

  • • Design a full 50-minute flow within the 1-Apparatus constraint.
  • • Command the room from start to finish.

Clearance Gate

Pass the Architect's V.A.E. (Validate, Align, Elevate) debrief. Upon success, you are cleared for the official Optimum Fit roster.
